Happy to share a new publication led by Arthur Masson, together with Sylvain Prigent, Ludovic Leconte, Léo Maury, Jean Salamero and Charles Kervrann.

This work introduces a new, redesigned Python-based architecture for BioImageIT, built to make bio-image analysis workflows easier to build, share and reproduce.

<strong>BioImageIT: A novel python-based architecture for reproducible bio-image workflows.</strong>
