## English

Happy to share our new paper, “DnaK1-mediated regulation of phototactic motility in Synechococcus elongatus UTEX 3055,” published in mLife.

We identify the molecular chaperone DnaK1 as an important regulator of movement toward light. DnaK1 interacts with the phototaxis regulator PixG and the motor protein PilB, and its loss impairs phototactic motility. These findings support a proposed model in which DnaK1 helps assemble or stabilize the protein complexes involved in this movement.

Congratulations to Shang-Yu Li and all our co-authors!

我们发现，分子伴侣 DnaK1 是细胞向光运动的重要调控因子。DnaK1 与趋光调控蛋白 PixG 和马达蛋白 PilB 相互作用，缺失 DnaK1 会损害趋光运动。这些发现支持一种假说模型：DnaK1 可能有助于相关蛋白复合物的组装或稳定。
